Workflow Blueprint

Our Strategic Mining-to-Export Pipeline

Step 01

Mining & Extraction

Extracted using safe techniques inside historic caverns. Experienced field engineers isolate maximum mineral grades, minimizing fracture faults in large block units.

Step 02

Quality Inspection

Each rock segment is scrutinized by quality supervisors. We strictly sort crystals by purity metrics, pink tone saturation, and size density.

Step 03

Processing & Crafting

Raw items are washed, air-dried, and custom milled. Experienced local craftsmen hand-carve luxury glowing lamps and cut precision salt building bricks.

Step 04

Moisture-Controlled Packaging

Secured inside double-barrier polymer bags to lock out marine moisture during transit. Rigid containers and wooden crates protect tiles and decorative shapes.

Step 05

Worldwide Port Export

Shipped through major seaports directly to Europe, Americas, Middle East, and Asia. Complete logistical documents, customs, and certificates sorted.
